# Summary

We measured the transfer function of all 52 tower-19 bolometers over 2.50–9.50 Hz (run RUNA,
71 drive steps). **All 52 respond above the quiet-data noise floor**, median SNR 109; 3.42 Hz is strongest on 42 of them.

Eleven modes are reported. Both regions the collaboration had hunted independently are corroborated, and no mode is a
pulse-tube harmonic of the measured 1.39443 Hz fundamental.

Three were found only by the blind verifier, yet sit in the analyst's own data on 44, 32 and
channels. Two analyst modes were withdrawn, one a reference-channel gain step. A mode both nodes
agreed on was demoted: they shared the faulty channel.

Damping was measured on seven modes by two independent methods, agreeing within 1.8σ on the five
both found; four gave honest non-detections. Causality is inconclusive: the pre-boundary signal equals the estimator's noise floor.

Partial and preliminary: one run, one axis, one tower.
